Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 25, 2026

Dupe Detective ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

What We Collect

  • Camera access (only when you choose to scan a receipt)
  • Receipt photos you scan (processed entirely on your device)

How We Use Your Data

All receipt scanning and duplicate detection happens locally on your iPhone. We do not upload, store, or share your receipt images or data with any servers or third parties.

Your Rights

You can delete all scanned data by uninstalling the app. You can revoke camera permission anytime in iPhone Settings → Dupe Detective.
