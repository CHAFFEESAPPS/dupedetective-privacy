The checklist

  1. Repeated lines. The same item and price on two lines usually means it was scanned twice. These are easy to miss when other items sit between them.
  2. Sale prices. Look for the shelf price on items you picked because of a sale. Loyalty-card discounts usually print as a separate line under the item.
  3. Coupons. Each coupon you handed over should appear as a negative amount.
  4. Weighed items. Produce and deli lines show a weight and price per pound. A bag of apples shouldn't weigh 4 pounds.
  5. Voids. If the cashier voided something, there should be a matching negative line.

Then check the total

The item count near the bottom should match what's in your bags. The total should match the card slip or the charge on your banking app.

Found a mistake?

Go to the register or customer service before you leave. It's much faster than calling later. If you're already home, read how to get a refund for a grocery store overcharge.
