How it works

Fix it at the register, not on the phone with your bank

A double charge takes a minute to fix while you're still in the store. After you leave, it means calling the store or disputing it with your bank and waiting days or weeks.

  1. Scan before you leave

    Tap Scan Receipt and point your iPhone at the receipt at the counter or on the way to the door. You can also import a digital receipt. It takes a few seconds.

  2. See what was charged twice

    Any item that appears more than once is highlighted in red, so a double scan is easy to spot on even a long receipt.

  3. Get it fixed on the spot

    Show the highlighted line to the cashier or customer service. Every flagged receipt is also saved in Alerts, so nothing slips through.

Your grocery list and receipt history in one place

Make your list on your Mac, and it's on your iPhone when you get to the store. While you shop, your list scrolls across the top of the app like a stock ticker, so you can see what's left at a glance. Then check the receipt and keep it for returns and warranties.

Privacy

Your receipts stay yours

Receipts are read on your device, and scanning works without an internet connection. When you sync between your iPhone and Mac, the data goes to your own private iCloud database. We can't open it, and we don't keep a copy on our servers.

  • ScanningOn your device
  • SyncYour private iCloud database
  • Who can read itOnly you. The developers have no access.
  • CameraUsed only when you choose to scan
